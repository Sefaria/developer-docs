## Sefaria + TypeSpec: A Microsoft Hackathon Collaboration

September 19, 2024

Every year, Microsoft engineers have a week to work on any hackathon project they choose. This year, Avi Levin (Microsoft Backend Developer) chose to work with Sarah Kreitman (Sefaria Software Engineer) on using TypeSpec, a new language in development at MS, to expedite the interactive API reference on our the Developer Portal. TypeSpec was created to facilitate smoother API generation and organization, among other things, so this was a perfect match.

## Sefaria Engineer Noah Santacruz Presents at PyCon Israel 2024

September 16, 2024

At PyCon Israel, engineers and developers from across the country come together to share about their work. We're honored to have had an opportunity for our AI research lead, Engineering Manager Noah Santacruz, to present about Sefaria's use of LLMs at this fantastic conference. Noah spoke to an audience of 200+ tech innovators about how he went beyond KMeans, eventually combining sklearn and langchain in order to get his text clustering just right (and get rid of the Misc clusters at the same time).
